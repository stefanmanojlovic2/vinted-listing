# TASTE.md — your taste profile

> [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) loads this file before writing anything: how a listing sounds and what never
> goes in. It ships **neutral**. The parts that are just craft (format, length, what a buyer needs)
> are filled in and work from the first run. The parts that are voice
> (your closing line, your words, your rules) are blank, because they're yours, not the author's.
> You fill them in by rejecting what the skill writes. See [Build your voice](#build-your-voice).

## How the skill uses this file

- No `Confirmed-by:` line under the title means you haven't set it up yet. On the first write the
  skill asks the three questions under [Defaults](#defaults), writes the answers in, and adds the line.
- Every time you reject a line or state a rule ("no cigarettes", "never say vintage"), the skill
  writes it into this file: rejected lines with your reason, rules under "Never in a description".
  That's the whole training loop. Three items of rejecting is usually enough to sound like you.
- Everything below is editable. Cross out a rule you disagree with and the skill follows your version.

## The listing

Craft, not taste. These hold for most sellers, so they ship filled in.

- **Chill, not salesy.** Describe it the way you'd describe it to a friend. Only what a buyer needs.
- **Title: just the item.** Brand, type, colour or print. No size, no condition — Vinted has fields
  for those, and repeating them wastes the 100 characters.
- **Description: three short paragraphs**, a blank line between each:
  1. **The item**, max 2 sentences: what it is, colour, print, the details that make it this piece.
  2. **The closing line**, one line — optional, see below.
  3. **Size, condition, material**, max 3 short sentences: label size and how it really fits,
     condition, material if known.
- **Keep it short.** No paragraph longer than the example below. Flaws are the only reason to go
  over: name every one and where it is, in a few words. "No visible flaws" only after checking
  every photo.
- **Language:** titles and descriptions in the listing language under Defaults. Hashtags may use
  local search words (`overhemd` on vinted.nl, `chemise` on vinted.fr).

Example of the shape (not of the voice — yours will read differently):

```
Levi's 501 straight jeans mid blue

Levi's 501 straight leg jeans in mid blue denim. Button fly, red tab on the back pocket.

[your closing line, if you use one]

Label size W32 L32, true to size. Good condition, light fading on the knees. 100% cotton.
```

## The closing line

One line in the middle of the description, and the only line with personality. It's optional: turn
it off in Defaults and the description is two paragraphs of plain facts, which sells fine.

If you use one, pick a shape and keep it consistent across items. Common ones:

- **Occasion + pairing** — "Perfect for [a real occasion or place]. Best paired with [plain everyday things]."
- **One plain use** — "Wears well with everything. The jacket I reached for most this winter."
- **Why you're selling** — "Bought it for a wedding, wore it once, it deserves better."

**What makes these work, whatever shape you pick:**

- Real, literal, specific things. A place you'd actually go, an object you'd actually own.
- One line. No punchline, no wink at the buyer.
- Vary it per item. The same joke on 30 listings reads like a template, because it is one.
- It has to sound like you saying it out loud. If you'd be embarrassed to text it to a friend, cut it.

**What usually fails,** as a starting point — replace these with your own once you've rejected a few:

- Luxury-lifestyle clichés (yachts, Grand Prix, casinos, ski chalets). Everyone writes them.
- Cute props and twists bolted on for charm. They read as written by a machine.
- In-jokes and references only you understand. Nobody gets them.
- One theme repeated across every item (every line a dating joke, every line food).

## Build your voice

The skill writes into these two lists as you react. Both ship empty on purpose.

**Approved** — lines you kept, so the skill can match them.

_(empty — your first kept lines land here)_

**Rejected, and why** — the more useful list. The reason matters more than the line.

_(empty — say why you don't like a line and it gets written here)_

How to fill them fast: write 3 items, read the closing lines, reject every one that doesn't sound
like you and say why in a few words. The next batch will be closer. Repeat once and it's usually there.

## Never in a description

Vinted conventions, not preferences — these either have their own field or put buyers off. Add yours
as you go.

- Care instructions
- "Smoke-free home"
- Bundle offers
- Shipping notes
- Measurement placeholders (Vinted has its own measurement fields)
